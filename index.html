01 / ABOUT

Research with purpose.
Technology with impact.

He is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at NIT Jalandhar, where his work spans computer networking, multimedia communication, network security, Internet of Things, and intelligent systems.

He received his Ph.D. from Malaviya National Institute of Technology Jaipur. Since joining NIT Jalandhar in 2019, He has been engaged in teaching, research, and academic activities. His research interests include developing efficient, secure, and adaptive computing and communication solutions for emerging applications.
